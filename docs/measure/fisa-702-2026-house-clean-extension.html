Clean extension of FISA Section 702, no reforms attached (S. 4465)

House · S4465 · April 30, 2026 · roll call 155

Passed261 yea · 111 nayOn Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass

House passage of S. 4465, extending the authorities of Title VII of FISA — including Section 702 — with no reform provisions attached. Leadership brought it up under suspension of the rules, which bars amendments, after a rule providing for consideration of a reform vehicle (H.R. 8035) failed on the floor two weeks earlier. A "clean" extension means the warrantless query authority continues exactly as it stood, with none of the warrant requirements that failed in 2024.

How this vote is scored. A yes vote on this measure counts as supporting surveillance; no counts as opposing it.

Verified against the bill text rather than the title: "extend the authorities of title VII" is an extension without reform, so a YEA vote continues warrantless querying. Do not infer direction from the word "amend" in the official title — it amends the 2008 FISA Amendments Act only to move the sunset date.
