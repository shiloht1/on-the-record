Reforming Intelligence and Securing America Act (RISAA) — House passage

House · HR7888 · April 12, 2024 · roll call 119

Passed273 yea · 147 nayOn Passage

Passage of H.R. 7888, reauthorizing Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act for two years. Despite the name, the bill passed without a warrant requirement for querying Americans' communications, after the Biggs amendment failed on a tie earlier the same day. It also broadened the definition of "electronic communication service provider," which critics argued swept in ordinary businesses that merely have access to communications equipment.

How this vote is scored. A yes vote on this measure counts as supporting surveillance; no counts as opposing it.

Paired with the Biggs amendment vote the same day. Members who voted for the warrant requirement and then against final passage have a consistent anti-warrantless-surveillance record; members who voted against the warrant requirement and for passage have a consistent record the other way.
