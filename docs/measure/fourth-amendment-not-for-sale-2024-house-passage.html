Fourth Amendment Is Not For Sale Act — House passage

House · HR4639 · April 17, 2024 · roll call 136

Passed219 yea · 199 nayOn Passage

Passage of H.R. 4639, barring federal law enforcement and intelligence agencies from purchasing Americans' personal data from data brokers when they would otherwise need a warrant, subpoena, or court order to obtain it. This is the federal vote closest in substance to the commercial surveillance market that Flock Safety, Axon, and the data-broker industry operate in: it addresses the practice of buying data the government could not lawfully demand outright. The bill passed the House but was not enacted.

How this vote is scored. A no vote on this measure counts as supporting surveillance; yes counts as opposing it. This one runs opposite to intuition: the measure restricts surveillance, so voting yes is a vote against it.

A YEA vote restricts government data purchases and is therefore anti-surveillance. The bill's House passage was bipartisan, and the companion Senate amendment (see risaa-2024-senate-append-fourth-amendment) failed 31-61, which is why the practice remains lawful.
