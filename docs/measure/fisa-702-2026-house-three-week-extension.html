Three-week stopgap extension of FISA Section 702 (H.R. 9238)

House · HR9238 · June 11, 2026 · roll call 221

Failed198 yea · 218 nayOn Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass

A stopgap bill extending Title VII FISA authorities to July 2, 2026, with no reforms, brought up under suspension of the rules two days before the authorities were set to lapse. It failed 198-218 — short of the two-thirds required under suspension, and short of a simple majority as well. Section 702 authority lapsed on June 13, 2026.

How this vote is scored. A yes vote on this measure counts as supporting surveillance; no counts as opposing it.

Another clean extension with no reform provisions, so a YEA vote continues warrantless querying. Lower significance than the April vote because a three-week stopgap is partly a procedural question about negotiating leverage; some members who support reform voted no specifically to force a reform package, and some who oppose reform voted no for unrelated reasons. Read alongside the member's other votes rather than on its own.

Source: Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives
