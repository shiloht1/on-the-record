Reforming Intelligence and Securing America Act (RISAA) — Senate passage

Senate · HR7888 · April 19, 2024 · roll call 150

Passed60 yea · 34 nayOn Passage of the Bill

Senate passage of H.R. 7888, reauthorizing Section 702 of FISA for two years. The Senate rejected every reform amendment offered that day, including a warrant requirement for U.S. person queries (42-50) and an amendment appending the Fourth Amendment Is Not For Sale Act (31-61), before passing the bill unamended. Signed into law April 20, 2024.

How this vote is scored. A yes vote on this measure counts as supporting surveillance; no counts as opposing it.
