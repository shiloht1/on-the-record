Warrant requirement for searching Americans' communications (Biggs amendment to RISAA)

House · HR7888 · April 12, 2024 · roll call 114

Failed212 yea · 212 nayOn Agreeing to the Amendment

Amendment offered by Rep. Andy Biggs to H.R. 7888 requiring the government to obtain a warrant before querying Section 702 data for the communications of U.S. persons. Failed on a 212-212 tie — under House rules a tie defeats the amendment, so the reauthorization proceeded without a warrant requirement. This is the single most direct recorded federal test of whether a member supports warrantless access to Americans' communications.

How this vote is scored. A no vote on this measure counts as supporting surveillance; yes counts as opposing it. This one runs opposite to intuition: the measure restricts surveillance, so voting yes is a vote against it.

A YEA vote here is a vote FOR requiring a warrant, i.e. against warrantless surveillance. Scoring this the same direction as the passage vote would be exactly backwards. The amendment drew support from across both parties, which is why it is a high-signal vote rather than a party-line one.
