The vendors

The companies selling surveillance technology to American government agencies. Ownership matters here: a Fusus real-time crime center is an Axon product, and a Vigilant plate-reader network is Motorola’s. Agencies and news coverage often still use the acquired brand name.

Dropping one vendor is not the same as ending the program. Denver, Syracuse, and Durham all cancelled or declined Flock Safety contracts in 2026 and signed with Axon instead. The cameras stayed; the logo changed. Local contract terms — retention period, audit requirements, whether a national cross-agency lookup database is included — matter more than which company won.

Axon Enterprise (AXON)

Scottsdale, Arizona

TASERs, body cameras, the Evidence.com platform, Draft One AI report writing, and — since acquiring Fusus in February 2024 — real-time crime centers that fuse ALPR, gunshot detection, body cameras, drones, and private camera feeds into a single view. Increasingly the vendor that cities move to after dropping Flock, which means dropping Flock does not by itself end camera surveillance.

Babel Street

Location data and open-source intelligence, including the Locate X product built on commercially purchased phone location data.

Cellebrite (CLBT)

Mobile device extraction tools that pull data off seized phones. Widely deployed at local agencies, frequently without a public procurement debate.

Clearview AI

New York, New York

Face recognition built on billions of images scraped from the public web without consent. Subject of regulatory penalties in multiple countries.

Flock Safety

Atlanta, Georgia

The largest dedicated ALPR vendor in the United States, selling pole-mounted plate readers to police departments, HOAs, and businesses, with a nationwide cross-agency lookup network. The default target of most local camera fights.

www.flocksafety.com

Genetec

Montreal, Quebec

AutoVu ALPR and Security Center video management.

LexisNexis Risk Solutions

Accurint and related products sold to law enforcement and ICE.

Magnet Forensics

Maker of Magnet AXIOM, digital forensics software for extracting and analyzing data from phones, computers, and cloud accounts. Note for readers who arrived searching for "Axiom": this is the product with that name. It is distinct from Axon Enterprise, which is the camera and real-time-crime-center vendor.

Motorola Solutions (MSI)

Chicago, Illinois

Enterprise public-safety vendor. Owns Vigilant Solutions and Digital Recognition Network (commercial ALPR data), and Avigilon (video analytics). Typically the incumbent where an agency wants everything integrated.
